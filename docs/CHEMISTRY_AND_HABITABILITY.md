# Chemistry and measurement notes

[Back to the project overview](../README.md)

The cartridge has a dry-soil chamber, a soil-water pH tube, and two planned reagent tubes. The current sketch reads only raw moisture and pH inputs. It does not calculate pH, chemical concentrations, or habitability scores.

## Measurement channels

| Channel | Preparation or work needed |
| --- | --- |
| Soil moisture | Keep the sample separate from liquids and calibrate the probe for the test soil. |
| pH | Record the soil-to-water ratio and calibrate the interface with standard buffer solutions. |
| Nitrate/nutrient | Select a suitable validated test method and document its volumes, reaction time, and measurement range. |
| Salt/perchlorate | No assay is specified. Treat this as an unconfigured demonstration channel until a suitable method is selected and validated. |

Tube color alone does not identify nitrate, salinity, or perchlorate. Each reported measurement needs a defined method and calibration.

## Color-sensor calibration

1. Use fixed illumination and tube placement, shielded from room light.
2. Record dark and reagent-blank readings.
3. Record RGB and clear-channel readings for known standards within the method's range.
4. Check the calibration with separate test samples and flag values outside its range.

The sketch does not yet read the RGB sensor or position the two reagent tubes for separate readings.

## Interpreting results

Preserve raw readings alongside any future calibrated values. Any screening categories must state their test conditions and basis. They are not evidence of life or proof that an environment is habitable.

Follow the selected reagent supplier's handling and disposal instructions. Keep used cartridges separate from fresh samples.
